# Product requirements

Fill every section before the milestone starts. Leave the headings as they are.

## Summary

One sentence: who plays, and what they finish.

## Timebox

How many months the team stays on this project, and the date the feature-complete build is due.

## Player fantasy

What the player is, who they are with, and what kind of place they move through.

## Core loop

The repeated steps from the start of a session to the ending. Name the win condition.

## Pillars

The few rules that decide what gets made and what gets cut.

## Content

The places, characters, and beats that must ship. Mark anything that is optional.

## Pipeline

Where art is made, how it is exported, and what stays in the game engine (gameplay, animation state machines, and the shipping shell).

## Out of scope

What this project will not build.

## Milestones

A table: when, milestone name, and the sentence that means it is done.

## Success

How the team knows the project is finished.
