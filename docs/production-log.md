# Production log

What each recorded week put into the build, from the January start through the July release.

## 20 January 2025 — Start

The project is on. The whole art team moves onto it. Four months are reserved. The aim is a micro-game and a Blender-to-Godot path: paper sets and characters made in Blender, played in Godot.

## 27 January 2025 — Let's go

An early gameplay prototype runs. The level plan and the goals are sketched. Look development is in Blender. Animation cycles for both characters are explored and previewed as a Blender emulation. The technical director starts the scene-assembly pipeline and sends a first look-dev scene into Godot.

## 3 February 2025 — Pre-production

Character designs are tested in a new prototype build, still in the test environment. Creek surfaces and the willow are blocked. More scanned textures come in so environment work does not stall.

## 10 February 2025 — Assembling the game

The first real export path works: assets nest in Blender, sets and pieces export separately, and Godot receives them. Core navigation logic is ready to test. The weekly build is an obstacle course plus the first assembly of the vertical-slice level. An animation-tree sketch exists. Character topology is being finished for texturing.

## 17 February 2025 — Trees and hills

The world level is blocked in rough. Stone walls close off part of the map. Assets are re-exported with support loops and weighted normals instead of sharpness data. Snowman concepts are in review. The vertical slice is about two weeks away.

## 24 February 2025 — Going procedural

Slice assets and animations are in. Paths, deep snow, and the creek can be dressed procedurally. Layout-rig animation moves onto the final rigs. A full animation tree is in the game. The pipeline is in daily use. The leash collision path gets an optimization pass. Remaining slice textures are painted and scanned.

## 3 March 2025 — Vertical slice

Pre-production ends. The slice shows the intended look and feel. The action system between the two characters is rebuilt so they can interact with props. Animation cycles are adjusted so states flow. Intro storyboards and a start-menu design pass begin. The next two months are feature completion, art completion, and polish.

## 17 March 2025 — Music

Intro and ending are in storyboard and animation. An adaptive soundtrack and a music manager land in the build, with emote bubbles and a camera-magnet test. Clearing navigation and snow (stamina, falling) get playtest passes. Gate and branch rigs start. Texture export now lands files in the right place, without duplicate copies.

## 31 March 2025 — Beginning and ending

The week is the intro and the outro. A build contains the intro and the menus. Outro animation is blocked through several versions. Stretchy IK is replaced where the engine cannot import it: two bones track each other instead of one bone scaling. The hub set is dressed, with path crossings and signs. Playtesting continues.

## 7 April 2025 — Effects and lighting

The intro is implemented as the join between the main menu and play, and as the tutorial. Snow effects and shrub topple go in. Lighting is redone with more local lights after dropping a heavier global solution. The gate challenge on the east side of the map starts. Character textures wrap up. The outro gets a polish pass. Bird rigs start.

## 14 April 2025 — Snow and ice

Footstep effects, more environment reactions, bush wiggle, and item pick-up animation. The mouse cursor is redone. Ice sliding is in. Snuggle and gate-try clips are in review.

## 22 April 2025 — Polish, plus fish

The team is in polish: fix and improve, with a few animations still to land. Fish are added anyway.

## 28 April 2025 — Birds

Birds are in the woods. Petting between the two characters is finished. Cuddling is in the game. Intro prompts are polished. The rest of the week is bug fixing and preparing the early-access build.

## 19–20 May 2025 — Feature complete

The four-month plan is done. The game is feature complete and still has bugs and performance work. Subscribers can play. A store page is next. The public launch waits on that pass.

## 11 July 2025 — Public release

The game is free on the store pages. Early-access notes have been burned down into fixes. A first public patch follows within the same month for snow walking, shovel detection, and uneven-ground jitter.

## After release

A later patch reduces motion sickness and eases play. The production log itself stops at the pre-release entry. Patches are release notes, not weekly production logs.
